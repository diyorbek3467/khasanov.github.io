## 🔬 Research Overview

My research spans nuclear technology, environmental radioactivity, accelerator physics, and Monte Carlo radiation transport simulations. I develop and apply modern computational and experimental techniques for radiation measurements, dosimetry, and nuclear instrumentation — with a strong focus on real-world applications in environmental safety and particle accelerator technology.

---

## ☢️ Environmental Radioactivity & Radiological Risk Assessment

Systematic assessment of natural and artificial radionuclides in Uzbekistan and beyond:

- Natural radionuclide profiles (U-238, Th-232, Ra-226, K-40) in soil, rocks, and water
- Radiological risk indices: radium equivalent, external hazard index, annual effective dose
- Radioactivity of reservoirs and groundwater (Kattakurgan, Sobirsoy, Kattakurgan)
- Soil contamination at former underground nuclear explosion sites (Pomuk gas field)
- Radon (Rn-222) flux density and indoor radon hazard in rural settlements
- Heavy metal contamination correlated with radioactivity data

**Key tools:** HPGe gamma spectrometry, field measurements, radiological hazard models

---

## ⚛️ Accelerator Physics & Electron Beam Irradiation

Development of irradiation technologies using electron beam accelerators:

- Designed a **microtron-type electron beam accelerator setup** for irradiation of solid-state and biological samples
- Developed an **analytical dose calculation model** for electron beam dosimetry (validated experimentally)
- Calculation of exposure and absorbed dose via electron bremsstrahlung radiation
- Applications in radiation processing, food irradiation, and sterilization

**Ph.D. Thesis:** *Research on electron beam irradiation technology based on microtron accelerator and development of dose calculation algorithm* (IMP-CAS, 2024)

---

## 💻 Monte Carlo Radiation Transport Simulations

High-fidelity simulations for complex radiation environments:

- **Radiation shielding design** for HIAF (Heavy Ion Accelerator Facility) and proton therapy facilities using **FLUKA**
- **Induced radioactivity** analysis in cooling water, treatment rooms, and structural components
- Leakage neutron spectra measurements and calculations from U-238 and Pb targets
- Individual dose evaluation for occupational exposure in heavy-ion and proton therapy gantry rooms
- Radiation safety assessments for SIMM, HIAF, and Varian ProBeam Proton Therapy systems

**Code used:** FLUKA Monte Carlo

## 🔭 Neutron Activation Analysis (NAA)

Elemental and isotopic characterization via neutron activation:

- Elemental composition of **iron slags** from archaeometallurgical excavations in Uzbekistan
- **Trace elements in phosphate materials** and their environmental fate
- Neutron activation analysis of **rice samples** (MIT-Samarkand MISTI collaboration)
- Activity determination of K-40 and Cs-137 in perennial trees

**Collaboration:** MIT-Samarkand (MISTI) program; IAEA-funded projects

## 🤖 AI & Machine Learning in Nuclear Physics

Emerging research at the intersection of nuclear science and data science:

- **AI-driven isotope detection:** deep learning applied to gamma spectral analysis
- **Machine learning-based predictive modeling** of groundwater nitrate contamination
- **GSProcessor** — open-source software for batch processing of scintillation gamma-spectra
