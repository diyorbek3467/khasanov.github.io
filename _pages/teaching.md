## 👨‍🏫 Teaching Overview

I am currently an Associate Professor at the Engineering Physics Institute, Samarkand State University (since September 2025), where I deliver undergraduate courses in nuclear and radiation physics. My teaching philosophy emphasizes connecting theoretical foundations with practical laboratory experience and real-world applications in nuclear technology and radiation safety.

---

## 📚 Current Courses (Samarkand State University)

---

### Interaction of Particles and Radiation with Matter
**Level:** Undergraduate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Since:** September 2025

Covers the fundamental mechanisms by which charged particles, photons, and neutrons interact with matter:

- Stopping power and range of charged particles (heavy ions, electrons, protons)
- Bragg peak and its applications in particle therapy
- Photon interactions: photoelectric effect, Compton scattering, pair production
- Neutron moderation, absorption, and activation
- Attenuation coefficients and buildup factors
- Practical applications in radiation shielding and dosimetry

---

### Fundamentals of Atomic and Nuclear Physics
**Level:** Undergraduate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Since:** September 2025

Foundation course in modern atomic and nuclear physics:

- Atomic structure and quantum mechanical models
- Nuclear models: liquid drop, shell model
- Radioactive decay laws: alpha, beta, gamma emission
- Nuclear reactions and cross-sections
- Fission and fusion processes
- Introduction to nuclear energy and reactor physics
- Nuclear instrumentation and detection principles

---

### Dosimetry
**Level:** Undergraduate &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Since:** September 2025

Comprehensive course on radiation dosimetry principles and instrumentation:

- Fundamental quantities: absorbed dose, kerma, exposure, equivalent dose, effective dose
- Dosimetry systems: ionization chambers, TLDs, OSLDs, semiconductor detectors, film dosimeters
- Calibration methods and traceability to national standards
- External and internal dosimetry
- Radiation protection principles: justification, optimization (ALARA), dose limits
- Regulatory framework: IAEA Basic Safety Standards
- Practical laboratory sessions with HPGe and NaI(Tl) detectors

---

## 🧪 Laboratory & Practical Training

Students in my courses gain hands-on experience with:

- **Gamma spectrometry** using HPGe and NaI(Tl) detectors
- **Radiation detector calibration** and efficiency analysis
- **Monte Carlo simulations** (FLUKA / Geant4) for radiation transport problems
- **Data analysis** using Python and OriginLab
- **Environmental radioactivity measurements** — field and laboratory techniques
